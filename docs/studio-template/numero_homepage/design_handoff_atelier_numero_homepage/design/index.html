— The Source · Alashan, Inner Mongolia

A fibre finer
than thought.

For four generations, the herders of the Alashan plateau have combed — never sheared — the under-fleece of the capra hircus during the brief two-week window that follows the first thaw of April.

What they gather, by hand and at altitude, measures fourteen-and-a-half microns. It is what makes the Adèle coat fall the way it does, and the Mira knit feel like nothing at all.

— Words by Sora Aoki · Photography by Wen Li
